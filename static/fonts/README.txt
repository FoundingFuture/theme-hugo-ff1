  bricolage-grotesque.woff2       Bricolage Grotesque, the display face.
                                  Ateliers Mathieu Triay.
  bricolage-smallcaps.woff2       A small-capitals cut of the above, made
                                  for this theme. Every character with an
                                  uppercase form is a capital taken from a
                                  heavier instance and scaled to 85%, so
                                  its stroke lands at 92% of a full
                                  capital's rather than at 85%. Built by
                                  scripts/build-smallcaps.py, which shows
                                  its working.
  instrument-sans-latin.woff2     Instrument Sans, the reading face.
  ibm-plex-mono-400.woff2         IBM Plex Mono, for labels and counts.
  asimovian-latin.woff2           Asimovian, for the line beside the mark.

The small-capitals cut is a modification of Bricolage Grotesque. Its
original copyright notice is kept inside the font, as the licence
