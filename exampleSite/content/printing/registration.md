---
title: Registration, and why it is hard
date: 2026-07-28
description: Paper moves between passes, and every colour has to land anyway.
tags: [precision, measurement, geometry]
---

Paper takes up moisture from the ink and grows, by a fraction of a
millimetre and not evenly. Print the second colour on a sheet that is no
longer the size it was and the two will not sit on top of each other.

The trade's answer was to print wet on wet where it could, and where it
could not, to let the sheet settle for a day and accept the loss.
