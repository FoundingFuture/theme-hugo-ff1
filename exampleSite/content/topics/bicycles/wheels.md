---
title: A wheel held together by tension
date: 2026-08-03
description: The spokes below the hub do the carrying, by pulling less.
tags: [geometry, failure, standards]
---

A tensioned wheel does not hang from its upper spokes. Every spoke pulls
inward at rest, and the load is carried by the few at the bottom pulling
slightly less hard than before.

Which is why a wheel with one loose spoke is not a wheel missing one
support. It is a wheel with one place where the tension no longer adds up.
