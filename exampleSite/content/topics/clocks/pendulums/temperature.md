---
title: Compensating for temperature
date: 2026-08-14
description: Steel grows in summer, and a clock that ignores it loses a minute a month.
tags: [materials, temperature, precision, measurement]
---

A steel rod a metre long grows about a tenth of a millimetre for every ten
degrees. That is enough to cost a clock most of a minute over a month, which
was unacceptable to anyone selling accuracy.

The gridiron solves it by alternating steel and brass so their expansions
cancel. The mercury jar solves it by letting a column of mercury rise as the
rod lengthens, moving the centre of mass back up.
