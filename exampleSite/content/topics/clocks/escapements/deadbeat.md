date: 2026-08-18
description: Graham's answer, and the reason regulator clocks kept time.

Graham's version shapes the pallet faces so that one part of the surface is
concentric with the pallet's own pivot. While the tooth rests there it does
nothing at all: no push, no drag, no recoil.

{{< pull >}}The wheel is held, and the pendulum is left alone.{{< /pull >}}

That is the whole idea, and it is worth about a second a week.
