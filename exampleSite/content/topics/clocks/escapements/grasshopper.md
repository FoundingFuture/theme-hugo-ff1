---
title: Harrison's grasshopper
date: 2026-08-19
description: An escapement with no sliding contact anywhere, and so nothing to oil.
tags: [friction, precision, materials]
---

Harrison's problem was that oil thickens with cold and thins with heat, so a
clock lubricated at the bench keeps different time at sea. His answer was to
build an escapement where no surface slides against another at all.

The pallets pivot in and out of the wheel rather than dragging across it.
Nothing rubs, so nothing needs oil, so nothing changes when the temperature
does.
