---
title: The anchor escapement
date: 2026-08-21
description: Simple, forgiving, and it interferes with the pendulum the whole time.
---

The anchor is the one most people picture. Two pallets on a rocking arm, a
toothed wheel, and a tick that carries across a room.

It is easy to make and easy to repair, which is why it outlived better
ideas. Its fault is that the pallets stay in contact with the wheel through
the swing, so the train is pushing the pendulum for most of its arc rather
than at the moment it would do least harm.
