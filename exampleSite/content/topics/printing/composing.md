---
title: Setting to measure
date: 2026-08-01
description: Every line the same width, because the frame will not hold otherwise.
tags: [standards, measurement, manufacturing]
---

Type is set to a fixed measure and every line has to fill it exactly. A line
left short will not lock up, and a forme that will not lock up falls apart
when it is lifted.

Justification is not an aesthetic in metal. It is what stops the page
becoming a heap.
