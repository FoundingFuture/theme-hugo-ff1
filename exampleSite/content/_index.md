title: FoundingFuture I
---

{{< lead >}}A Hugo theme for deep content. Folders become the menu, at any depth. Search, tags and related pages are built in and need no service.{{< /lead >}}

```toml
theme = "foundingfuture-i"
```

{{< items >}}
Search | no server | Compiled into the build. A title or a tag beats a body match
Tags | narrowing | Click one, then another. Tags that lead nowhere disappear
Near this | related | Other pages sharing its tags, best match first
On this page | contents | Three headings or more gets a contents list
Further down | recursive | A topic page lists its subtopics' pages too
Previous and next | reading | Walk a topic end to end without going back
Every heading | anchored | Copy a heading's link, send someone straight to it
Every word | i18n | Change one word. Hugo merges your file over ours
Six faces | self-hosted | Served from your domain. No CDN, no consent banner
No framework | plain CSS | Two small scripts, both for finding things. Pages work without them
{{< /items >}}
