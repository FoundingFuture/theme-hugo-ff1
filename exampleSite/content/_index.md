{{< lead >}}Your folders are the menu. Your headings are the contents. Your tags are the index. Set `theme` and build.{{< /lead >}}

{{< columns >}}
The menu reads `site.Home.Sections`. Your folders keep the names you gave
them, at whatever depth you took them. Add one tomorrow and it turns up on
its own.

`_index.md` makes a section, so it joins the menu. `index.md` makes a page,
and the files beside it become its downloads. That is Hugo's rule. Learn it
once and you own the menu.

{{< pull >}}Rows that open wear small capitals. The lettering tells you which way is deeper.{{< /pull >}}

#### Dense on purpose

Short measure. Tight rows. One colour per topic, meaning the same thing on
every page it lands on. Put a lot of text on one screen and it still reads.

The small capitals are real glyphs. Ask a browser to fake them and it
shrinks the capitals, leaving the stroke weight behind. Ours holds one
weight end to end.
