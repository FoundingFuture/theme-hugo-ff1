---
title: Why aluminium needs bigger tubes
date: 2026-08-07
description: A third the stiffness, so the tube has to grow to make it back.
tags: [materials, geometry, manufacturing]
---

Aluminium is about a third as stiff as steel for the same shape. Stiffness
rises with the fourth power of the tube's diameter, so a modest increase in
girth buys it all back and then some.

That is why an aluminium frame looks fat next to a steel one. It is not
strength, it is geometry doing the work the material cannot.
