---
title: Gearing, in inches
date: 2026-08-05
description: Gear inches, a unit that outlived the high-wheel bicycle it was invented to describe.
tags: [measurement, standards, geometry]
---

Gear inches describe the wheel a penny-farthing would need to travel the
same distance per pedal revolution. The machine is a century gone and the
unit is still on every chart, because it answers the question riders
actually ask.
