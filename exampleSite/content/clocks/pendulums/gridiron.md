---
title: The gridiron pendulum
date: 2026-08-12
description: Nine rods of two metals, arranged so their expansions cancel.
tags: [materials, temperature, manufacturing, precision]
---

Steel grows with heat and so does brass, about half as much again. Alternate
them so the brass pushes the bob up by exactly what the steel lets it down,
and the effective length stops moving.

It takes nine rods and a great deal of filing to get right, which is why the
mercury jar replaced it wherever someone was paying by the hour.
